How to print

  1. Load US Letter paper or cardstock and open the PDF.
  2. In the print dialog, set scale to 100% / “Actual size”. Turn off “Fit to page” — otherwise every card prints the wrong size.
  3. Cut along the thin grey lines. The short marks in the margin line up with every cut, so you can line up a ruler or paper trimmer.
